Hand-drawn chalk arrows, numbered 1 to 16 in Black and White and 1 to 13 in Red, Purple and Green. Use one arrow to point at a key number or idea. The brand guide says arrows stay `black-oak` (or White on dark grounds); the coloured sets exist for one-off creative use only. Never stretch, distort or recolour. Arrow 9 (zig-zag up) is the default growth arrow, 3 is a loop or refresh, 4 is two-way, 7 is a cycle. Arrow 17 (TIF) was left out.
