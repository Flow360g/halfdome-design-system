The six soft-cornered brand shapes on a 6300 x 4200 canvas. Colourways: Red (`sequoia`) 1 to 5, Purple (`camas`) 2 to 6, Green (`white-fir`) 1 to 6, Black 1 to 6 and White 1 to 6. Green 6 is supplied in portrait orientation. Use one shape per page, never rotated onto an angle. Images always sit inside or on a shape: cut-out imagery sits on a coloured shape, and rectangular photos are masked to the Black version (see the ShapeImage component). Black and White shapes are for masks and for one-colour production, not as decorative fills.
