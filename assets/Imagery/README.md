The brand's playful 3D object imagery: everyday things given a twist (a banana phone, a rocket-powered trolley, a lemon on holiday). Every file is a cut-out on transparency, made to sit on a brand shape.

- Place each image on one coloured shape (`ShapeImage`), one per page. Never rotate it or put it in a plain rectangle.
- Pick the image for the idea: HD_CHOC_GRAPH or HD_LEGO for growth and building, HD_ROCKET_SHOPPING_ARROW and HD_TROLLEY for ecommerce, HD_MAGNIFY_DOME for insight and audits, HD_PENCIL and HD_BULB for ideas, HD_CHESS for strategy, HD_GOLDENCALCULATOR for budgets, HD_BLOWTHINGSUP for the "Blow things up." value, seasonal ones (HD_CHRISTMAS_PEAR, HD_HOLIDAY_LEMON, HD_HAPPYBIRTHDAY_DISCO) for occasions.
- Need something that is not here? The brand guide says to ask the design team rather than sourcing stock.

Seven of the largest originals (HD_BALLOON_DOG, HD_DONUT_DUMBBELL, HD_FLUFFY_WATERMELON, HD_FLYING_BASKET, HD_HOUSEOFCARDS, HD_PINEAPPLE, HD_TURTLE) are over the transfer limit and remain in SharePoint (Branding > Asset Library > Imagery), as do the HiBob and Cake + Numbers sub-folders. HD_SUPERMAN was left out because it shows a licensed character.
