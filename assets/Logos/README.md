The halfdome wordmark: lowercase geometric letters with the "o" replaced by a half dome. File names read Halfdome-Logo-<word colour>-<dome colour>; all are transparent PNGs at 3120 x 660.

- **Light grounds**: `Halfdome-Logo-Black-Red` (default), `-Black-Purple`, `-Black-Green`, or `-Black` (single colour).
- **Dark or photo grounds**: `Halfdome-Logo-White-Red`, `-White-Purple`, `-White-Green`, or `-White`.
- **Coloured wordmarks** (`Red-*`, `Purple-*`, `Green-*`) and the reversed-dome pairs (`Black-White`, `White-Black`) are alternates for posters and merchandise; use sparingly and never purple or gold wordmarks on light grounds, where they fail contrast.
- "White" in these files is `snow` (#F7F6F5), not pure white.
- Vector EPS/AI masters are in SharePoint (Branding > Logos > EPS Files and WORKING FILES).
