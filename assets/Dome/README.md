The standalone half dome, the brand icon. One file per brand colour: `Dome-Red` (`sequoia`), `Dome-Purple` (`camas`), `Dome-Green` (`white-fir`), `Dome-Black` (`black-oak`), `Dome-White` (`snow`, for dark grounds). Use for avatars, favicons, sign-offs and as a cover anchor. Transparent PNGs at 1800 x 960. On section dividers without an image, and on the closing slide, a large dome (about 560px wide at 1920 x 1080) sits on the bottom edge at the right margin, in a colour that contrasts with the ground.
