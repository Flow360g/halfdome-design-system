# Logo

Places the official halfdome wordmark from the uploaded logo files; never rebuild it in type.

**Props**
- `ink`: `"black"` (default, light grounds), `"white"` (dark or photo grounds) or `"red"` (the alternate red wordmark with a black dome; use sparingly).
- `dome`: `"red"` (default), `"purple"`, `"gold"` or omit for single-colour. Match it to the page's accent colour.
- `height`: rendered height in px (default 32). Keep at least 24px on screen.

**Do** give it clear space equal to the dome's height on every side. **Don't** stretch, rotate, recolour or place it on a busy part of an image.
