# Voice and values

## The name and the idea

Half Dome is named after the granite rock face in Yosemite: the founders, Joe, Tom and Will, joked about starting a business together while camping beneath it on a West Coast road trip, years before the agency existed. The name was meant to be a placeholder and stuck. The palette names echo that landscape: Sequoia, Camas, White Fir, Snow and Black Oak.

**Whole Potential.™** is the brand platform. It articulates how the agency already behaves and where it wants to get to, and it guides OKRs, client work and how the team talks about Half Dome with clients, partners and the industry.

## Writing in the voice

- **Confident, not loud.** Say what happened and what it means. "Prospecting did the heavy lifting this quarter." beats "Amazing results!!"
- **Plain and specific.** Name the channel, the number and the period. Explain any metric a client might not know.
- **Warm.** We, you and first names. A light touch of humour is welcome in internal and social work, never at a client's expense.
- **Short headlines.** Six to ten words, sentence case. Statements are two or three words with a full stop.
- **Australian English** throughout.
