# Data visualisation

Half Dome reports are read by marketers making budget decisions; charts must be honest, quick and on-brand.

- **Series order**: `chart-1` (`sequoia`) for the client or the hero series, `chart-2` (`black-oak`, `snow` on dark) for the comparison or benchmark, then `chart-3` (`camas`) and `chart-4` (`white-fir`, with a `black-oak` outline on light grounds). Two series is the norm; four is the maximum.
- **Gridlines** in `chart-grid`, three at most. No chart borders, 3D, gradients or shadows.
- **Labels** in `caption` (12px) and `ink-muted`; direct-label the key bar or point instead of relying on the legend.
- **Change**: show deltas with ▲ / ▼ and a sign in `positive` / `negative`. Never rely on red versus not-red alone.
- **Hero numbers** go in `StatTile`, one emphasised tile per row at most.
- **Point at the insight** with one chalk `Arrow`, not with colour.
- **Diagrams** follow `assets/Diagrams/HD_MESSYMIDDLE.png`: thin lines, dotted guides, two brand hues.
- Always state the source and period under the chart in `caption`: "Source: Meta Ads Manager, 1 Jul to 30 Sep."
